About Psymora

Built in Nigeria, for the gap we kept running into ourselves.

Psymora Ltd is a CAC-registered Nigerian company working across AI evaluation, psychometrics, behavioral analytics, and human-AI interaction.

Our products all start from the same observation: proven psychological measurement tools exist and work well abroad, but rarely reach the clinics, research labs, and schools that need them here.

We don't invent new psychometric science. We build the infrastructure that makes existing, validated science usable: locally, affordably, and without asking a clinician, a researcher, or a parent to trust a black box.

We're starting in Nigeria because it's the gap we understand best, but the same gap exists across much of Africa, and that's the direction we're building toward.

How we decide what to build

A few rules we don't bend.

Validated frameworks, always

Every product is built on an established, peer-reviewed instrument or framework, never a test we've invented ourselves.

AI touches the plumbing, not the judgment

Reminders, summaries, digitization: yes. Scoring, diagnosis, clinical or academic judgment: no, not without a dedicated research partner and real validation.

Narrow, on purpose

We'd rather be excellent at one workflow than mediocre across ten. Scope creep is the thing we say no to most often.
